Goal: Produce a board-ready research brief on the global fintech complaints landscape

Researcher uses two AI models (one writes, one critiques) before producing a single polished output. Try Model Council if you have time.

Step 1 · Open ResearcherM365 COPILOT CHAT
In Copilot Chat, switch to the Researcher agent. Keep the model selector on Auto (Critique mode). Optionally switch to Model Council for a comparative cover letter.
Step 2 · Run the prompt
I am a Director of Sectoral Supervision at Contoso Financial Services Authority. Produce a 2-page research brief on global trends in peer-to-peer lending consumer complaints in the last 18 months. Cover: (a) the most common complaint themes; (b) regulatory responses across at least 4 jurisdictions (UK FCA, Australia ASIC, Singapore MAS, India RBI); (c) emerging supervisory practices around collection-conduct and fee transparency; (d) three implications for an Indonesian-style regulator overseeing 80 licensed P2P entities. Use authoritative sources (regulators, IMF, BIS, FSB). End with a 3-bullet "What this means for Contoso FSA" section.

Click Export to Word. This becomes the briefing pack input for Exercise 4.

5 minPremiumWORD COPILOT02 ContosoFSA_Supervisory_Meeting_Transcript.docx

Goal: Turn a supervisory review transcript into a formal meeting minutes document

In real life this comes from Teams Recap auto-transcription. Today we use a sample transcript so everyone — including those who did not attend a real meeting — can run the exercise.

Step 1 · Open the fileWORD ON THE WEB
Download 02 ContosoFSA_Supervisory_Meeting_Transcript.docx and open it in Word. Click the Copilot icon in the ribbon.
Step 2 · Generate formal minutes
Transform this meeting transcript into a formal Contoso FSA meeting minutes document. Use the following structure: (1) Header — meeting title, date/time, location, classification; (2) Attendees in a clean list; (3) Key decisions in bullet form; (4) Discussion summary, organised by topic (Banking, Capital Markets, Fintech, Consumer Protection, Surveillance) with 2-4 bullets each; (5) Action items in a table with columns: Action / Owner / Due date; (6) Next meeting date placeholder. Use neutral, authoritative regulator tone. Bahasa Indonesia headers, English-or-Indonesian content as in the source.
Step 3 · Polish
Ask Copilot: "Now make sure all action items have a clear owner and a date. Highlight any item that is missing one in red in a follow-up note."

Tips & promptsTake these home

Five prompt habits that make Copilot 10× better

  • Set the role. "Act as a senior risk analyst..." beats no role 9 times out of 10.
  • Set the audience. "...for a board pre-read" or "...for the Director" changes the output.
  • Demand structure. Ask for bullets, sections, tables — Copilot will obey.
  • Constrain the length. "In 6 bullets" or "in 200 words" prevents wall-of-text.
  • Iterate. First draft is rarely the final. Ask Copilot to "make it more concise" or "make the tone more authoritative".

Three things Copilot will not do for you

  • Make supervisory or enforcement decisions — Copilot drafts, you decide.
  • Replace primary-source verification — always cross-check facts before publication.
  • Access systems it has not been connected to — your tenant, your files, your rules.
